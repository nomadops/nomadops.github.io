Services

Agents, and everything they need to work safely.

We develop DevOps and SRE agents, build MCP servers and operations tools into your app, and help your team improve its agentic DevOps and SRE posture.

01 · Agents

DevOps and SRE agents

We develop our own DevOps and SRE agents, and we work with the agents your team already uses.

  • HeyPennyGeneral-purpose fixer
  • InspectorGadgetFinder and debugger
  • AtlasBuilds pipelines
  • Setup and connection of the SRE and DevOps agents your team already uses
  • Tests for agent behavior, built from your own past incidents
  • Approval steps and logs for every agent action
  • SRE agents
  • DevOps agents
  • MCP

02 · MCP

MCP servers for your app

MCP servers (connectors that let AI agents use your app's tools), made for SRE and DevOps work.

  • Read tools for health, logs, metrics, deploys and queues
  • Action tools such as retry, restart and roll back, each with its own approval rule
  • Access per agent and per tool, and a log of every call
  • Works with any agent that supports MCP

03 · Apps

Operations tools and panels in your app

Useful operations tools, built directly into your app, for your team and your agents.

  • Health and status panels for each part of your app
  • Admin actions with confirmation steps and an audit log
  • Agent activity and approvals in one place
  • The same actions for people in the panel and for agents through MCP

04 · Teams

Agentic DevOps and SRE posture

Help for companies and teams to improve how well agents can see, run and fix their product safely.

  • A review of your product, pipelines and operations: what agents can see and do today
  • A plan for access, approval steps, runbooks, logs and alerts
  • Shared setup and review rules for the agents your engineers use
  • Workshops and pairing sessions on real work from your backlog

05 · Infrastructure

Infrastructure as code

Cloud infrastructure and delivery pipelines that people and agents can change safely.

  • Infrastructure as code with Terraform and Pulumi
  • CI/CD pipelines with a plan or preview check on every change
  • Custom Terraform modules for patterns you repeat
  • Terraform
  • Pulumi

How a project works

Start small, then widen.

  1. Step 1

    Assess

    We review your product's agentic DevOps and SRE posture, and choose the first jobs for agents.

  2. Step 2

    Build

    We build in your repositories and your app, through your review process. You see every change.

  3. Step 3

    Hand over

    We document everything and train your team, so you own and run what we built.
