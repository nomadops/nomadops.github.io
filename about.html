About us

Nomadic engineers building agents for DevOps and SRE.

We are a small team of seasoned engineers. We started with infrastructure as code and CI/CD. Now we develop DevOps and SRE agents, build MCP servers and operations tools for apps, and help teams get ready for agents.

Why nomadops?

We work from everywhere.

We are a company of digital nomads. We travel and work remotely from all around the world. Most often you find us in Istanbul. Otherwise we work from cafes in Amsterdam, Copenhagen, Barcelona, San Francisco and beyond.

What do you do?

Process first, then agents.

Great tools are the result of operational excellence, not the other way around. That is even more true for agents. An agent is only as safe as the access, approvals and logs around it, so we build those first. We work with Terraform, Pulumi and many SRE and DevOps agents.

What else?

We build micro-startups.

We turn tools we build for our own problems into small products. Current work: profiles.dev, a GitHub App to show your developer profile, and terraform.careers, a job board.

Why a Corgi?

Corgis kept us going.

nomadops started during the Covid lockdowns, when Corgi videos did a lot for our mental health. The Corgi does not have a name yet. Send us your suggestions.
